IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

Do Not Use MY LISAA If Any of the Following Apply to You

  • Under 18 years of age.
  • Pregnant.
  • Breastfeeding an infant under 6 months old.
  • Meningitis.
  • Kidney or liver disease of any kind.
  • Have only one kidney.
  • Have had your spleen removed.
  • Sickle cell anemia.
  • History of bleeding in the brain (brain hemorrhage).
  • Familial Mediterranean Fever (FMF).
  • Epilepsy.
  • Cancer, including individuals who have recovered from cancer.
  • Heart disease or rapid heartbeat/tachycardia.
  • History of serious blood clots.
  • High blood pressure.
  • Have undergone stomach/gastric surgery — wait at least 18 months after surgery before use.
  • Have undergone abdominoplasty/tummy-tuck surgery — wait at least 18 months after surgery before use.
  • Have undergone a minor surgical procedure — wait at least 2 months after surgery before use.
  • Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
  • Panic attacks/panic disorder.
  • Taking antidepressant medication at a dose of 100 mg or higher.
  • Currently using antibiotics, corticosteroid-containing medicines, blood thinners, or cold/cough medications.
  • Osteoporosis.
  • Multiple sclerosis (MS).

Medication & Health Warning: Customers with any medical condition or who take prescription or over-the-counter medication should speak with a qualified healthcare professional before use.

Keep out of reach of children. Do not exceed the directions stated on the product label.
